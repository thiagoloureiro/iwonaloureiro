# About

## Iwona Loureiro

Interior designer and architect. Graduate of the Faculty of Architecture and Urban Planning at the Cracow University of Technology. Licensed for unlimited architectural design in Poland.

## Background

Founded IL Studio after 13 years working as an architect in Kraków. Interior design became the primary focus — combining professional experience, aesthetic sensitivity, and a desire to create meaningful spaces.

## Approach

Spaces we live in affect well-being. IL Studio creates interiors that are functional, comfortable, visually pleasing, and tailored to each client's lifestyle. Art — painting, drawing, and graphic design — continues to inspire the work.
