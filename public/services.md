# Services

Interior design services offered by Iwona Loureiro Studio.

## Consultation

Initial meeting to discuss expectations, vision, needs, inspirations, and lifestyle.

## Concept Design

Functional 2D layouts divided into zones, plus interior visualizations showing proposed colors, materials, furniture, and lighting.

## Detailed Design

Execution-ready package including functional plans, lighting layouts, sections and elevations, custom furniture drawings, equipment specifications, and final visualizations.

## Author Supervision

On-site support during construction, material and color selection assistance, and ensuring implementation matches documentation.
